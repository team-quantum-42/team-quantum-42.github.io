ROBOCUP JUNIOR · RESCUE LINE

Team Quantum 42

Building, testing, breaking, and rebuilding robots to compete in RoboCup Junior Rescue Line.

We are a North-India based team participating in the RoboCup Junior Rescue Line league. The team started in 2024 with four original members: Raghav, Adhrit, Meher, and Abhiram.

During the 2024–2025 season, we achieved 15th place at India Nationals, 15th place at North-India Regionals, and received the Best Hardware Solution award at the Regional competition.

THE ROBOT

Built for the line.

Our robot is designed to navigate the Rescue Line course autonomously, combining sensors, control systems, mechanical design, and plenty of testing.
